The blueprint is deliberately simple to state and hard to execute well: **target
a 12–15% annual yield tied to large-cap equities**, generated by selling covered
calls against a portfolio of productive, high-implied-volatility assets, and
distribute that yield as income.[^yield]

## Where the yield comes from

Yield is not conjured; it is harvested. Each leg of the program contributes
premium:

- **A large-cap equity core.** Liquid, deep-options underlyings (the QQQ complex
  and its constituents) anchor the book and keep the overwrite tradable at scale.
- **Covered calls around quantum and AI infrastructure.** The frontier-tech
  names carry the richest implied volatility in the market, which means the
  fattest premium per unit of exposure.
- **Disciplined distribution.** Collected premium, net of hedging and costs, is
  passed through to investors as a regular income stream.

## Why covered calls on these names

Owning the underlying makes the call "covered" — our obligation is always
backed by the asset, so we are never naked short volatility. When a name is
called away, we have sold an asset we owned into strength; when it is not, we
keep both the shares and the premium. Either outcome advances the income mandate.

The art is in *which* names, *which* strikes, and *how much* of each position to
overwrite — and that is the work of the [Quant Engine](/quant-engine/).

## The opportunity set

The blueprint spans several baskets, each chosen because it pairs a real,
ownable asset with structurally elevated implied volatility:

- **Frontier tech** — quantum and AI infrastructure
- **Space** — launch and orbital infrastructure
- **Crypto miners** — compute-heavy, high-beta operators
- **Robotics** — automation and embodied-AI exposure

The [Frontier Tech Baskets](/frontier-baskets/) chapter details the names.
