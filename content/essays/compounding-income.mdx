At the core of our strategy is a belief in the long-term strength of American
innovation.

Few financial assets have demonstrated greater wealth creation than the Nasdaq.
Through crashes, recessions, bubbles, wars, financial crises, and periods of
extreme uncertainty, the underlying engine of innovation has continued to move
forward.

The path has never been smooth.

The dot-com crash erased years of gains. The Global Financial Crisis shook
confidence in the entire financial system. The COVID panic created one of the
fastest market declines in history.

Yet over time, innovation prevailed.

The lesson is not that markets never decline.

The lesson is that productive businesses continue creating value, and over long
periods, markets have historically recovered from even the most severe setbacks.

This belief forms the foundation of our portfolio.

We seek exposure to innovation while generating income through disciplined option
strategies, including covered calls.

Covered calls are not a new invention. For generations, investors have used
option premiums to generate cash flow from assets they already own. The strategy
exchanges a portion of upside potential in return for immediate income and
improved cash generation.

Human Instinct embraces this philosophy as a foundation for long-term capital
allocation.

Our objective is not to maximize returns in every market environment.

Our objective is to generate durable cash flow while maintaining exposure to
long-term innovation.
