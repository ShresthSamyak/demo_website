We believe artificial intelligence represents one of the largest infrastructure
investments in modern history.

The market often focuses on the applications.

We focus on the ecosystem.

Every breakthrough model requires data centers. Every data center requires
power. Every power system requires equipment, cooling, networking, construction,
and physical infrastructure.

The scale of this investment cycle may ultimately rival previous infrastructure
revolutions including electrification, telecommunications, and the internet.

Human Instinct seeks opportunities throughout this ecosystem.

We are particularly interested in businesses that become increasingly important
as AI adoption expands, especially those that provide critical infrastructure
rather than consumer-facing products.
