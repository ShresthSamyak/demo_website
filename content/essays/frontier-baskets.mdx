The premium we distribute is only as rich as the volatility we own. These
baskets are selected for exactly that: real, productive assets at the frontier
of compute, each carrying structurally high implied volatility that a systematic
overwrite can monetize.[^names]

## Quantum

The earliest, most volatile layer of next-generation compute.

- **IFNQ** — quantum-computing exposure.

## Space

Launch cadence and orbital infrastructure — capital-intensive, high-beta, and
deeply optionable.

- **ASTS** — space-based connectivity.
- **RKLB** — launch and space systems.

## Crypto miners

Compute-heavy operators whose equity volatility tracks both hardware cycles and
digital-asset prices — a double source of implied volatility.

- **HIVE** — digital-asset mining and HPC.

## AI infrastructure

The physical layer of intelligence: power, and the compute that consumes it.

- **BE** — Bloom Energy (on-site power for AI loads).
- **CLSK** — compute / mining infrastructure.

## Robotics

Automation and embodied AI, accessed through a liquid ETF wrapper.

- **BOTZ** — robotics & AI ETF.

## How the baskets are used

Each basket is a pool of overwrite candidates, not a static holding. The
[Quant Engine](/quant-engine/) decides, per name and per cycle, how much to
overwrite and at which strikes — concentrating the program where the
implied-to-realized spread is widest.
