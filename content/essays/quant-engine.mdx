Everything upstream is opportunity; the Quant Engine is execution. It turns a
universe of volatile, ownable assets into a repeatable income stream by deciding
— continuously and without emotion — *what to sell, where, and how much.*

## 1. Volatility forecasting

We forecast realized volatility per name and compare it to what the options
market is implying. Premium is only worth selling where implied sits
meaningfully above our realized forecast; the engine ranks the universe by that
edge and steers the overwrite toward it. Machine-learning models refine these
forecasts as new data arrives.

## 2. Strike optimization

Given a forecast, the engine selects strikes that maximize expected income net
of assignment risk.

- **LEAP options on QQQ** anchor the longer-dated structure, providing a stable
  base layer of premium and exposure.
- We lean on the underlying's drift characteristics: **QQQ has finished up at
  least 5% in roughly 85% of quarters**,[^stat] which shapes how aggressively we
  can sell upside without surrendering the bulk of participation.

## 3. Dynamic overwrite percentages

We do not overwrite a fixed fraction of the book. The **overwrite percentage is
dynamic** — scaled up when implied volatility is rich and forecast drift is
modest, and pulled back when the reverse is true. This is the single most
important lever for balancing income against upside capture, and it is set by
the model, not by hand.

## Replicating and extending the income ETFs

A proven reference set already exists in the market. We **build algorithms that
replicate the NEOS-style income products — QQQI, XQQQI, SPYI** — and then extend
the same machinery across our own baskets:

- Reproduce the index-overwrite income profile of the established products.
- **Apply the same strategies across the frontier baskets** to generate
  incremental income.
- **Collect premium across AI infrastructure** specifically, where implied
  volatility is highest.

The goal is not to copy a single product but to own the *method* — a portable
overwrite engine we can point at any liquid, volatile, optionable asset.
