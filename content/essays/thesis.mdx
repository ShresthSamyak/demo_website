Markets are not merely collections of cash flows. Markets are collections of
people — and people, in aggregate, pay a great deal to avoid uncertainty.[^vrp]

That willingness to overpay for protection is the most durable inefficiency we
know of. It does not depend on predicting direction, picking winners, or timing
tops. It depends only on the fact that implied volatility, across most assets
and most of the time, prints richer than the volatility that is actually
realized. **Human Instinct exists to harvest that spread, systematically, and to
distribute it as income.**

## A systematic high-income strategy

Human Instinct is a quant-enhanced, systematic covered-call income strategy,
enhanced with machine learning and automation. We do not discretionarily guess
which calls to sell. We forecast volatility, optimize strikes, and size our
overwrite programmatically — then let the same engine run across every basket we
own.

The strategy is built in two phases:

- **Phase 1 — Income Strategy.** Stand up the systematic covered-call engine on
  liquid, large-cap underlyings and prove the income stream.
- **Phase 2 — A tokenized quantitative income fund** delivering institutional-style
  covered-call yield to global investors, with the access and transparency that
  tokenization makes possible.

## Own productive assets. Own the volatility.

Most income products force a choice: own boring, low-volatility assets and
collect thin premium, or own exciting, high-volatility assets and collect no
income at all. We reject the trade-off.

Our principle is to **own productive assets — and specifically the volatile
compute infrastructure** powering the AI and frontier-technology buildout — and
then **monetize their high implied volatility** through disciplined call
overwriting. The same names whose price swings frighten buy-and-hold investors
are, to a systematic seller of options, the richest source of premium available.

We are not betting against these assets. We own them. We simply ask them to pay
us rent while we wait.
