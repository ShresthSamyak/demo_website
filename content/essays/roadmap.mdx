A thesis is only as good as its evidence and its execution. The final pieces of
the blueprint are validation and the software to run it live.

## Portfolio back-testing

Before any strategy trades capital, it is **back-tested** across regimes — bull,
bear, and the high-volatility shocks that most threaten an income book — to
characterize yield, drawdown, and assignment behavior.

- **Testfol.io** is used for portfolio back-testing and rapid iteration on
  allocation and overwrite assumptions.

## Software buildout

The strategy is automation-first. We are building the execution platform on:

- **Alpaca** — the brokerage and API platform for building, automating, and
  running the systematic overwrite program end to end.

The software layer is what turns the [Quant Engine](/quant-engine/) from a model
into a fund: forecasting, strike selection, order routing, hedging, and
distribution, all automated.

## The road ahead

- **Phase 1 — Income Strategy.** Prove the systematic covered-call income stream
  on the core book.
- **Phase 2 — Tokenized income fund.** Deliver institutional-style covered-call
  yield to global investors through a tokenized vehicle.
