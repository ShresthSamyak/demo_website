The investment industry has spent decades pursuing a simple goal: remove human
emotion from decision-making.

Algorithms replaced intuition. Models replaced judgment. Data replaced
experience.

Yet despite unprecedented access to information, markets remain governed by the
same force that has driven them for centuries: human behavior.

Human Instinct was founded on a simple observation. Markets are not merely
collections of cash flows, balance sheets, and valuation metrics. Markets are
collections of people.

People experience fear. People experience greed. People follow narratives.
People chase momentum. People panic during uncertainty and become euphoric
during booms.

As technology advances, these behaviors do not disappear. They simply operate at
a larger scale.

We believe understanding human behavior remains one of the most durable edges in
investing.
